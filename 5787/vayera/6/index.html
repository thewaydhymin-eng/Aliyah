6th Aliyah

Parashat Vayera 5787 · 6th Aliyah · Bereishit 21:22–34 · Friday 30 October 2026

Boker Tov! May today’s Aliyah give us the courage to speak honestly and make peace that lasts.

Avraham would not swear friendship over an unspoken wrong.

Avimelech and Pichol, the commander of his army, come to Avraham and tell him that Elohim is with him in all he does, and they ask him to swear not to deal falsely with them. Avraham agrees, but first he reproves Avimelech about a well that Avimelech’s servants had seized, and Avimelech says he had not heard of it until that day. The two men make a covenant, and Avraham sets apart seven ewe lambs as a witness that he dug the well, so the place is called Beer-Sheva. Avraham plants a tamarisk there, calls on the Name of יהוה, El Olam, and lives many days in the land of the Philistines.

1. What did Avimelech see in Avraham? (21:22)

Avimelech and Pichol told Avraham that Elohim was with him in all he did.

The testimony comes from outsiders, not from Avraham himself.

In Gerar Avraham had thought there was no fear of Elohim in that place (20:11). Now its king sees Elohim with him.

ActionLive today so that the people around you can see whom you serve.

Choose one ordinary area where outsiders watch you: your work, your money or your word. Keep it with integrity today, especially where no one would notice if you cut a corner.

2. Why did Avraham raise the matter of the well before swearing the oath? (21:24–30)

Avraham agreed to swear, but first he reproved Avimelech about the well his servants had seized.

Avimelech said he had not heard of it until that day.

Naming the wrong made an honest covenant possible, and the seven ewe lambs became the witness that Avraham dug the well.

ActionName the unresolved matter before you renew the relationship.

Before you sign, agree or reconcile, identify the issue that is still unspoken. Raise it calmly and clearly, and settle it in a way both sides can witness.

3. How does Yeshua teach us to deal with a wrong done to us? (21:25; Mattityahu 18:15)

Yeshua teaches that if your brother sins against you, you must go and show him his fault between you and him alone.

Avraham took the matter of the well directly to Avimelech.

Direct words, spoken in time, protect the relationship that gossip would poison.

ActionSpeak to the person directly, not about them.

If you have told others about a wrong done to you, stop. Go to the person privately this week, explain what happened, and listen to their side.

Action of the DayPeace that hides the well will not hold.

Choose one unspoken grievance you have been smiling over. Today, arrange to speak to that person directly and privately. Name the matter plainly, listen to their answer, and agree on what will make it right.

Parashat Vayera 5787 · 6de Aliyah · Bereishit 21:22–34 · Vrydag 30 Oktober 2026

Boker Tov! Mag vandag se Aliyah ons die moed gee om eerlik te praat en vrede te maak wat hou.

Avraham wou nie vriendskap sweer terwyl ’n onreg onuitgespreek bly nie.

Avimeleg en Pigol, die bevelhebber van sy leër, kom na Avraham en sê vir hom dat Elohim met hom is in alles wat hy doen, en hulle vra hom om te sweer dat hy nie vals teenoor hulle sal optree nie. Avraham stem in, maar eers berispe hy Avimeleg oor ’n put wat Avimeleg se dienaars met geweld geneem het, en Avimeleg sê hy het eers daardie dag daarvan gehoor. Die twee manne sluit ’n verbond, en Avraham sit sewe ooilammers eenkant as getuie dat hy die put gegrawe het, en daarom word die plek Be’er-Sheva genoem. Avraham plant daar ’n tamarisk, roep die Naam van יהוה, El Olam, aan en woon baie dae in die land van die Filistyne.

1. Wat het Avimeleg in Avraham gesien? (21:22)

Avimeleg en Pigol het vir Avraham gesê dat Elohim met hom is in alles wat hy doen.

Die getuienis kom van buitestaanders, nie van Avraham self nie.

In Gerar het Avraham gedink daar is geen vrees vir Elohim op daardie plek nie (20:11). Nou sien die koning dat Elohim met hom is.

AksieLeef vandag só dat die mense om jou kan sien wie jy dien.

Kies een gewone terrein waar buitestaanders jou dophou: jou werk, jou geld of jou woord. Hanteer dit vandag met integriteit, veral waar niemand sal agterkom as jy ’n draai sny nie.

2. Hoekom het Avraham die saak van die put geopper voordat hy die eed afgelê het? (21:24–30)

Avraham het ingestem om te sweer, maar eers het hy Avimeleg berispe oor die put wat sy dienaars met geweld geneem het.

Avimeleg het gesê hy het eers daardie dag daarvan gehoor.

Om die onreg te benoem het ’n eerlike verbond moontlik gemaak, en die sewe ooilammers het getuie geword dat Avraham die put gegrawe het.

AksieBenoem die onopgeloste saak voordat jy die verhouding hernu.

Voordat jy teken, ooreenkom of versoen, identifiseer die saak wat nog onuitgespreek is. Opper dit kalm en duidelik, en los dit op ’n manier op wat albei kante kan getuig.

3. Hoe leer Yeshua ons om op te tree wanneer iemand ons onreg aandoen? (21:25; Mattityahu 18:15)

Yeshua leer dat as jou broer teen jou sondig, jy moet gaan en hom sy fout wys tussen jou en hom alleen.

Avraham het die saak van die put direk na Avimeleg geneem.

Direkte woorde, betyds gespreek, beskerm die verhouding wat skinder sou vergiftig.

AksiePraat direk met die persoon, nie oor hulle nie.

As jy ander vertel het van ’n onreg wat jou aangedoen is, hou op. Gaan hierdie week privaat na die persoon, verduidelik wat gebeur het, en luister na hulle kant.

Aksie van die DagVrede wat die put wegsteek, sal nie hou nie.

Kies een onuitgesproke grief waaroor jy nog net glimlag. Reël vandag om direk en privaat met daardie persoon te praat. Benoem die saak duidelik, luister na hulle antwoord, en kom ooreen wat dit sal regstel.
