Parashat Vayetze 5787 · 6th Aliyah · Bereishit 31:17–42 · Friday 20 November 2026

Neither Good nor Bad

Boker Tov! May today’s Aliyah show us that no hand can harm us when Elohim has spoken.

Lavan pursued Yaakov for seven days, and one word from Elohim in the night held back his hand.

While Lavan is away shearing his sheep, Yaakov sets his family on camels and leaves for Kna’an without telling him, and Rachel steals her father’s terafim. Lavan pursues for seven days and overtakes him in the hills of Gil’ad, but Elohim comes to Lavan in a dream and warns him not to speak to Yaakov either good or bad. Lavan accuses Yaakov of stealing away and of stealing his gods, and searches every tent, but Rachel hides the terafim in the camel’s saddle and sits on them. Yaakov then answers Lavan with twenty years of faithful service, ewes that never miscarried, losses he bore himself, heat by day and frost by night, and says that if the Elohim of his father had not been with him, Lavan would have sent him away empty.

1. What did Elohim tell Lavan? (31:24, 29)

Elohim came to Lavan in a dream by night and told him to take care not to speak to Yaakov either good or bad.

Lavan admits it himself: it was in the power of his hand to do harm, but the Elohim of Yaakov’s father had spoken.

Seven days of pursuit were undone by one sentence in the night.

ActionWrite down the “Lavan” who seems to hold power over you.

Under it write Bereishit 31:29. Pray that Elohim will speak where you cannot.

2. What do Rachel’s terafim reveal? (31:19, 30–35)

Rachel stole her father’s terafim, Lavan called them his gods, and they lay hidden in a camel’s saddle with Rachel sitting on them while Lavan searched.

Lavan’s gods had to be stolen, carried and hidden. They could not even be found by their owner.

Idols had come along into the camp of the man who met Elohim at Beit-El.

ActionSearch your own “tent” for something that does not belong in a home that serves יהוה.

An object, a habit or a source of security. Remove it before Shabbat.

3. What does the Haftarah say Yisra’el must confess about idols? (31:30; Hoshea 14:4)

In the Haftarah Yisra’el is told to return and to say that they will no longer call the work of their hands their gods.

Lavan accused Yaakov of stealing his gods. Hoshea teaches Yisra’el to confess that the work of their hands is not their god.

Gods that can be stolen were made by hands. יהוה made the hands.

ActionRead Hoshea 14:2–4 aloud as a prayer.

Replace the general words with your own specifics: name what you will no longer call your help.

4. Do I flee in fear instead of trusting Elohim to speak? (31:20, 31)

Yaakov left without telling Lavan, and when Lavan asked why, Yaakov said he had been afraid that Lavan would take his daughters by force.

יהוה had told Yaakov to return. Fear decided how he left.

Elohim protected him anyway, but the secrecy made Lavan’s accusation possible.

ActionHave one honest conversation you have been avoiding out of fear.

Prepare your words in prayer. Speak plainly and leave the outcome to Elohim.

5. Will I be faithful when no one is checking? (31:38–40)

Yaakov said that for twenty years Lavan’s ewes and goats had not miscarried, he had not eaten Lavan’s rams, he bore the loss of what was torn, and he endured heat by day and frost by night.

Most of that faithfulness happened in the fields, at night, unseen.

Yaakov’s defence was not words but twenty years of work.

ActionFinish one unseen task today with full care before Shabbat begins.

Choose something no one will inspect, and do it as if Elohim is watching, because He is.

Action of the DayLavan had the power of his hand. Elohim had the last word.

Before Shabbat begins tonight, go through your home and remove one object, habit or source of security that competes with יהוה, and say aloud that it is no longer your help.

Parashat Vayetze 5787 · 6de Aliyah · Bereishit 31:17–42 · Vrydag 20 November 2026

Nie Goed of Sleg Nie

Boker Tov! Mag vandag se Aliyah ons wys dat geen hand ons kan skade aandoen wanneer Elohim gepraat het nie.

Lavan het Yaakov sewe dae lank agtervolg, en een woord van Elohim in die nag het sy hand teruggehou.

Terwyl Lavan weg is om sy skape te skeer, sit Yaakov sy gesin op kamele en vertrek na Kna’an sonder om hom te vertel, en Rachel steel haar vader se terafim. Lavan jaag hom sewe dae lank agterna en haal hom in die berge van Gil’ad in, maar Elohim kom in ’n droom na Lavan en waarsku hom om met Yaakov nie goed of sleg te praat nie. Lavan beskuldig Yaakov dat hy weggesluip en sy gode gesteel het, en deursoek elke tent, maar Rachel steek die terafim in die kameel se saal weg en sit daarop. Yaakov antwoord Lavan dan met twintig jaar se getroue diens, ooie wat nooit gemis het nie, verliese wat hy self gedra het, hitte bedags en ryp snags, en sê dat as die Elohim van sy vader nie by hom was nie, Lavan hom met leë hande sou weggestuur het.

1. Wat het Elohim vir Lavan gesê? (31:24, 29)

Elohim het in ’n droom in die nag na Lavan gekom en vir hom gesê om op te pas dat hy met Yaakov nie goed of sleg praat nie.

Lavan erken dit self: dit was in die mag van sy hand om skade te doen, maar die Elohim van Yaakov se vader het gepraat.

Sewe dae van agtervolging is deur een sin in die nag ongedaan gemaak.

AksieSkryf die “Lavan” neer wat skynbaar mag oor jou het.

Skryf Bereishit 31:29 daaronder. Bid dat Elohim sal praat waar jy nie kan nie.

2. Wat openbaar Rachel se terafim? (31:19, 30–35)

Rachel het haar vader se terafim gesteel, Lavan het dit sy gode genoem, en dit het weggesteek gelê in ’n kameel se saal met Rachel wat daarop sit terwyl Lavan soek.

Lavan se gode moes gesteel, gedra en weggesteek word. Selfs hulle eienaar kon hulle nie vind nie.

Afgode het saamgekom in die kamp van die man wat Elohim by Beit-El ontmoet het.

AksieDeursoek jou eie “tent” vir iets wat nie hoort in ’n huis wat יהוה dien nie.

’n Voorwerp, ’n gewoonte of ’n bron van sekuriteit. Verwyder dit voor Shabbat.

3. Wat sê die Haftarah moet Yisra’el oor afgode bely? (31:30; Hoshea 14:4)

In die Haftarah word vir Yisra’el gesê om terug te keer en te sê dat hulle die werk van hulle hande nie meer hulle gode sal noem nie.

Lavan het Yaakov daarvan beskuldig dat hy sy gode gesteel het. Hoshea leer Yisra’el om te bely dat die werk van hulle hande nie hulle god is nie.

Gode wat gesteel kan word, is deur hande gemaak. יהוה het die hande gemaak.

AksieLees Hoshea 14:2–4 hardop as ’n gebed.

Vervang die algemene woorde met jou eie besonderhede: noem wat jy nie meer jou hulp sal noem nie.

4. Vlug ek uit vrees in plaas daarvan om Elohim te vertrou om te praat? (31:20, 31)

Yaakov het vertrek sonder om Lavan te vertel, en toe Lavan vra waarom, het Yaakov gesê hy was bang dat Lavan sy dogters met geweld sou wegneem.

יהוה het vir Yaakov gesê om terug te keer. Vrees het bepaal hoe hy vertrek het.

Elohim het hom tog beskerm, maar die geheimhouding het Lavan se beskuldiging moontlik gemaak.

AksieVoer een eerlike gesprek wat jy uit vrees vermy het.

Berei jou woorde in gebed voor. Praat reguit en laat die uitkoms aan Elohim oor.

5. Sal ek getrou wees wanneer niemand kyk nie? (31:38–40)

Yaakov het gesê dat Lavan se ooie en bokke twintig jaar lank nie gemis het nie, dat hy nie Lavan se ramme geëet het nie, dat hy die verlies van wat verskeur is self gedra het, en dat hy hitte bedags en ryp snags verduur het.

Die meeste van daardie getrouheid het in die veld gebeur, in die nag, ongesien.

Yaakov se verweer was nie woorde nie, maar twintig jaar se werk.

AksieVoltooi vandag een ongesiene taak met volle sorg voordat Shabbat begin.

Kies iets wat niemand gaan nagaan nie, en doen dit asof Elohim kyk, want Hy kyk.

Aksie van die DagLavan het die mag van sy hand gehad. Elohim het die laaste woord gehad.

Gaan voordat Shabbat vanaand begin deur jou huis en verwyder een voorwerp, gewoonte of bron van sekuriteit wat met יהוה meeding, en sê hardop dat dit nie meer jou hulp is nie.
