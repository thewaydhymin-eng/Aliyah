Parashat Toldot 5787 · 4th Aliyah · Bereishit 26:23–29 · Wednesday 11 November 2026

They Saw That יהוה Was With Him

Boker Tov! May today’s Aliyah put worship before everything we build.

The men who drove Yitzchak away came back because they saw יהוה with him.

Yitzchak goes up to Beer-Sheva, and that night יהוה appears to him as the Elohim of Avraham his father and tells him not to fear, because He is with him and will bless him. Yitzchak builds an altar there, calls on the Name of יהוה, pitches his tent, and his servants dig a well. Avimelech comes from Gerar with his adviser and the commander of his army, and Yitzchak asks why they have come, since they hated him and sent him away. They answer that they have clearly seen that יהוה is with him, and ask for an oath and a covenant of peace between them.

1. What did Yitzchak do after יהוה appeared to him at Beer-Sheva? (26:23–25)

He built an altar, called on the Name of יהוה, pitched his tent there, and his servants dug a well.

Altar first, tent second, well third.

Worship came before settling and before providing.

ActionPut worship first in the order of your day.

Before you open messages or start work tomorrow, spend the first ten minutes in prayer and the Word.

2. Why does יהוה say “Do not fear” at Beer-Sheva? (26:24)

After the strife over the wells, יהוה tells Yitzchak that He is the Elohim of Avraham his father, that He is with him, and that He will bless him for Avraham’s sake.

The promise is anchored in who יהוה has already been to his father.

Fear is answered with presence: I am with you.

ActionWrite down what יהוה has already been to those before you.

List three ways יהוה proved faithful to your parents, your teachers or the believers who brought you the truth. Read the list when fear rises.

3. How does Yeshua describe the witness others see? (26:28; Mattityahu 5:14–16)

Yeshua tells His disciples to let their light shine before others, so that they see their good works and glorify their Father in heaven.

Avimelech’s men did not quote Yitzchak’s claims. They described what they had seen.

Testimony is what others observe, not only what we say.

ActionRead Mattityahu 5:14–16 beside Bereishit 26:26–29.

Ask what the people around you could honestly say they have seen in your life this month.

4. Can those who opposed me see יהוה with me? (26:27–28)

Yitzchak’s former opponents said they had clearly seen that יהוה was with him.

His conduct after being sent away became the evidence.

Hostility tests what people can see in me.

ActionTreat someone who opposed you in a way that reflects יהוה.

Choose one person who has been against you. Do one fair, honest act toward them this week without expecting anything back.

5. Will I listen to the people who hurt me? (26:27–29)

Yitzchak named their hostility openly, and then listened to their proposal of peace.

He did not pretend the past had not happened.

He still let them speak.

ActionHear out one person who wronged you, with the past named honestly.

If someone who hurt you is reaching out, agree to talk. Say plainly what happened, then listen fully before you answer.

Action of the DayYitzchak built an altar before he dug a well.

Build your altar first today: before work, messages or plans, spend ten minutes in prayer and the Word, then begin the day.

Parashat Toldot 5787 · 4de Aliyah · Bereishit 26:23–29 · Woensdag 11 November 2026

Hulle Het Gesien dat יהוה by Hom Was

Boker Tov! Mag vandag se Aliyah aanbidding vóór alles plaas wat ons bou.

Die manne wat Yitzchak weggejaag het, het teruggekom omdat hulle gesien het dat יהוה by hom is.

Yitzchak trek op na Be’er-Sheva, en daardie nag verskyn יהוה aan hom as die Elohim van sy vader Avraham en sê vir hom om nie te vrees nie, want Hy is by hom en sal hom seën. Yitzchak bou daar ’n altaar, roep die Naam van יהוה aan, slaan sy tent op, en sy dienaars grawe ’n put. Avimeleg kom van Gerar af saam met sy raadgewer en die bevelhebber van sy leër, en Yitzchak vra hoekom hulle gekom het, aangesien hulle hom gehaat en weggestuur het. Hulle antwoord dat hulle duidelik gesien het dat יהוה by hom is, en vra vir ’n eed en ’n vredesverbond tussen hulle.

1. Wat het Yitzchak gedoen nadat יהוה by Be’er-Sheva aan hom verskyn het? (26:23–25)

Hy het ’n altaar gebou, die Naam van יהוה aangeroep, sy tent daar opgeslaan, en sy dienaars het ’n put gegrawe.

Eers die altaar, dan die tent, dan die put.

Aanbidding het gekom voordat hy hom gevestig en voorsien het.

AksieSit aanbidding eerste in die volgorde van jou dag.

Voordat jy môre boodskappe oopmaak of begin werk, bestee die eerste tien minute aan gebed en die Woord.

2. Hoekom sê יהוה by Be’er-Sheva: “Moenie vrees nie”? (26:24)

Ná die twis oor die putte sê יהוה vir Yitzchak dat Hy die Elohim van sy vader Avraham is, dat Hy by hom is, en dat Hy hom ter wille van Avraham sal seën.

Die belofte is geanker in wie יהוה reeds vir sy vader was.

Vrees word met teenwoordigheid beantwoord: Ek is by jou.

AksieSkryf neer wat יהוה reeds was vir dié wat voor jou gegaan het.

Lys drie maniere waarop יהוה getrou was aan jou ouers, jou leermeesters of die gelowiges wat die waarheid na jou gebring het. Lees die lys wanneer vrees opkom.

3. Hoe beskryf Yeshua die getuienis wat ander sien? (26:28; Mattityahu 5:14–16)

Yeshua sê vir sy dissipels om hulle lig voor ander te laat skyn, sodat hulle hul goeie werke kan sien en hul Vader in die hemel verheerlik.

Avimeleg se manne het nie Yitzchak se aansprake aangehaal nie. Hulle het beskryf wat hulle gesien het.

Getuienis is wat ander waarneem, nie net wat ons sê nie.

AksieLees Mattityahu 5:14–16 saam met Bereishit 26:26–29.

Vra wat die mense om jou eerlik kan sê hulle hierdie maand in jou lewe gesien het.

4. Kan dié wat my teengestaan het, sien dat יהוה by my is? (26:27–28)

Yitzchak se voormalige teenstanders het gesê dat hulle duidelik gesien het dat יהוה by hom is.

Sy optrede nadat hy weggestuur is, het die bewys geword.

Vyandigheid toets wat mense in my kan sien.

AksieBehandel iemand wat jou teengestaan het op ’n manier wat יהוה weerspieël.

Kies een persoon wat teen jou was. Doen hierdie week een regverdige, eerlike daad teenoor hulle sonder om iets terug te verwag.

5. Sal ek luister na die mense wat my seergemaak het? (26:27–29)

Yitzchak het hulle vyandigheid openlik benoem en toe na hulle voorstel van vrede geluister.

Hy het nie voorgegee dat die verlede nie gebeur het nie.

Hy het hulle steeds laat praat.

AksieLuister na een persoon wat jou onreg aangedoen het, met die verlede eerlik benoem.

As iemand wat jou seergemaak het, uitreik, stem in om te praat. Sê reguit wat gebeur het, en luister dan volledig voordat jy antwoord.

Aksie van die DagYitzchak het ’n altaar gebou voordat hy ’n put gegrawe het.

Bou vandag eers jou altaar: voor werk, boodskappe of planne, bestee tien minute aan gebed en die Woord, en begin dan die dag.
