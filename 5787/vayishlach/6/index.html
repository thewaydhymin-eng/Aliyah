6th Aliyah

Parashat Vayishlach 5787 · 6th Aliyah · Bereishit 35:11–36:19 · Friday 27 November 2026

From Ben-Oni to Binyamin

Boker Tov! May today’s Aliyah teach us to name our days by the promise of יהוה and not by our sorrow.

Rachel named her last son Ben-Oni, son of my sorrow, as she died; Yaakov named him Binyamin, son of the right hand.

Elohim tells Yaakov that He is El Shaddai, commands him to be fruitful and multiply, and promises that a nation and an assembly of nations and kings will come from him, and that the land given to Avraham and Yitzchak will be his. On the way to Efrat, which is Beit-Lechem, Rachel dies giving birth to a son; she names him Ben-Oni, but Yaakov names him Binyamin, and he sets up a pillar on her grave. Reuven lies with Bilhah, his father’s concubine, and Yisra’el hears of it; the twelve sons of Yaakov are listed, and Yitzchak dies at Chevron at a hundred and eighty years, and Esav and Yaakov bury him. The Aliyah closes with the generations of Esav, who is Edom, and how he moved away from Yaakov to Mount Se’ir because their possessions were too great for them to live together.

1. What did El Shaddai promise Yaakov at Beit-El? (35:11–12)

Elohim told him to be fruitful and multiply, that a nation and an assembly of nations and kings would come from him, and that He would give him and his seed the land He gave to Avraham and Yitzchak.

The promise made to Avraham now rests fully on Yaakov.

Kings are promised to a man who has just buried idols and is about to bury his wife.

ActionWrite down one promise of El Shaddai that you are still waiting to see.

Next to it, write what you see of it already. Thank Him for both.

2. Why did Yaakov change Rachel’s name for her son? (35:16–18)

As she was dying Rachel named her son Ben-Oni, but his father called him Binyamin.

Rachel named the child by her sorrow. Yaakov named him by strength and favour.

The father refused to let the boy carry the grief of his birth as his identity.

ActionStop calling one thing in your life by the name of its pain.

Choose a name for it that reflects what יהוה has promised, and use that name aloud this week.

3. How does the Brit Chadashah remember Rachel? (35:19; Mattityahu 2:16–18)

When the children of Beit-Lechem were killed, Mattityahu says the word of Yirmeyahu was fulfilled: Rachel weeping for her children and refusing to be comforted.

Rachel died on the road to Beit-Lechem. Her weeping is heard there again at the birth of Yeshua.

The same town holds her grave and the birthplace of the Messiah.

ActionRead Mattityahu 2:13–23 beside Bereishit 35:16–20, and Yirmeyahu 31:15–17.

Write down the promise יהוה gives Rachel in Yirmeyahu 31:16–17.

4. What is heard in my house that I think is hidden? (35:22)

Reuven lay with Bilhah, his father’s concubine, and the text says that Yisra’el heard of it.

The Torah records it in one short line and does not hide it.

What is done in secret is heard, and it returns in Bereishit 49:3–4.

ActionBring one hidden matter into the light this Erev Shabbat.

Confess it to יהוה, and to the person affected if it concerns them.

5. Can I stand beside my brother at our father’s grave? (35:29)

Yitzchak died old and full of days, and his sons Esav and Yaakov buried him.

The brothers who were once divided by their father’s blessing stand together at his burial.

Shared grief can bring together what rivalry pulled apart.

ActionContact one family member you are distant from before Shabbat begins.

Call or write today. Wish them Shabbat Shalom and ask how they are.

Action of the DayRachel named him by her sorrow. His father named him by the right hand.

Before Shabbat begins tonight, call or write to one family member you have grown distant from, wish them Shabbat Shalom, and ask how they are.

Parashat Vayishlach 5787 · 6de Aliyah · Bereishit 35:11–36:19 · Vrydag 27 November 2026

Van Ben-Oni na Binyamin

Boker Tov! Mag vandag se Aliyah ons leer om ons dae te noem na die belofte van יהוה en nie na ons smart nie.

Rachel het haar laaste seun Ben-Oni genoem, seun van my smart, terwyl sy sterf; Yaakov het hom Binyamin genoem, seun van die regterhand.

Elohim sê vir Yaakov dat Hy El Shaddai is, beveel hom om vrugbaar te wees en te vermeerder, en belowe dat ’n nasie en ’n vergadering van nasies en konings uit hom sal voortkom, en dat die land wat aan Avraham en Yitzchak gegee is, syne sal wees. Op pad na Efrat, wat Beit-Lechem is, sterf Rachel terwyl sy geboorte gee aan ’n seun; sy noem hom Ben-Oni, maar Yaakov noem hom Binyamin, en hy rig ’n gedenksteen op haar graf op. Reuven slaap by Bilhah, sy vader se byvrou, en Yisra’el hoor daarvan; die twaalf seuns van Yaakov word gelys, en Yitzchak sterf in Chevron op honderd-en-tagtig jaar, en Esav en Yaakov begrawe hom. Die Aliyah sluit af met die geslagte van Esav, wat Edom is, en hoe hy van Yaakov af weggetrek het na die gebergte Se’ir omdat hulle besittings te groot was om saam te woon.

1. Wat het El Shaddai by Beit-El aan Yaakov belowe? (35:11–12)

Elohim het vir hom gesê om vrugbaar te wees en te vermeerder, dat ’n nasie en ’n vergadering van nasies en konings uit hom sal voortkom, en dat Hy aan hom en sy nageslag die land sal gee wat Hy aan Avraham en Yitzchak gegee het.

Die belofte aan Avraham rus nou ten volle op Yaakov.

Konings word belowe aan ’n man wat pas afgode begrawe het en op die punt staan om sy vrou te begrawe.

AksieSkryf een belofte van El Shaddai neer waarop jy nog wag.

Skryf langsaan wat jy reeds daarvan sien. Dank Hom vir albei.

2. Waarom het Yaakov Rachel se naam vir haar seun verander? (35:16–18)

Terwyl sy sterf, het Rachel haar seun Ben-Oni genoem, maar sy vader het hom Binyamin genoem.

Rachel het die kind na haar smart genoem. Yaakov het hom na krag en guns genoem.

Die vader het geweier dat die seun die smart van sy geboorte as sy identiteit dra.

AksieHou op om een ding in jou lewe na sy pyn te noem.

Kies ’n naam daarvoor wat weerspieël wat יהוה belowe het, en gebruik daardie naam hierdie week hardop.

3. Hoe onthou die Brit Chadashah vir Rachel? (35:19; Mattityahu 2:16–18)

Toe die kinders van Beit-Lechem doodgemaak is, sê Mattityahu dat die woord van Yirmeyahu vervul is: Rachel wat oor haar kinders ween en weier om getroos te word.

Rachel het op pad na Beit-Lechem gesterf. Haar geween word daar weer gehoor by die geboorte van Yeshua.

Dieselfde dorp hou haar graf en die geboorteplek van die Messias.

AksieLees Mattityahu 2:13–23 saam met Bereishit 35:16–20, en Yirmeyahu 31:15–17.

Skryf die belofte neer wat יהוה in Yirmeyahu 31:16–17 aan Rachel gee.

4. Wat word in my huis gehoor wat ek dink verborge is? (35:22)

Reuven het by Bilhah, sy vader se byvrou, geslaap, en die teks sê dat Yisra’el daarvan gehoor het.

Die Torah teken dit in een kort reël aan en steek dit nie weg nie.

Wat in die geheim gedoen word, word gehoor, en dit kom terug in Bereishit 49:3–4.

AksieBring hierdie Erev Shabbat een verborge saak in die lig.

Bely dit aan יהוה, en aan die persoon wat geraak is as dit hulle aangaan.

5. Kan ek langs my broer by ons vader se graf staan? (35:29)

Yitzchak het oud en sat van dae gesterf, en sy seuns Esav en Yaakov het hom begrawe.

Die broers wat eens deur hulle vader se seën verdeel is, staan saam by sy begrafnis.

Gedeelde smart kan bymekaarbring wat wedywering uitmekaar getrek het.

AksieKontak een familielid van wie jy ver geraak het voordat Shabbat begin.

Bel of skryf vandag. Wens hulle Shabbat Shalom toe en vra hoe dit met hulle gaan.

Aksie van die DagRachel het hom na haar smart genoem. Sy vader het hom na die regterhand genoem.

Bel of skryf voordat Shabbat vanaand begin aan een familielid van wie jy ver geraak het, wens hulle Shabbat Shalom toe, en vra hoe dit met hulle gaan.
