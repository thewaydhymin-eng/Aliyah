Did Yahushua Commit Blasphemy by Claiming to Be the Incarnation of Elohim?

If Yahushua had claimed to be Elohim, his accusers would have found him guilty. What the trial accounts actually show.

4 min read3-page studyDownload PDF

The term blasphemy refers to saying something about Elohim that is disrespectful.

Did Yahushua claim he was the Great I AM? Did he claim he and יהוה were one being?

Christianity and even some Messianic groups would have us believe he did just that and the Yehudim tried him for blasphemy because of it. They say Yahushua was found innocent of blasphemy because he was יהוה so it wasn’t blasphemy. If Yahushua did in fact make those claims, then he would have been found guilty of blasphemy, but they found him innocent!

Christianity teaches that when asked by Pilot about these charges that Yahushua remained silent implying, he was Elohim. Yahushua did not reply to those charges because he had already provided a defence to the Yehudim and knew he must die at the hands of his brothers to save them. The truth is not that difficult to understand when Scripture and the words of Yahushua are not taken out of context they were meant. Yahushua did not claim to be יהוה he claimed to be the son of יהוה 87 times. Yahushua was simply declaring a fact that יהוה proclaimed of all of us:

Tehillim/Psalm 82

6I have said, you are (all) gods; and all of you are children of the most High.

The Yehudim in authority at that time were trying to arrest Yahushua for blasphemy claiming that by saying he was the son of יהוה was committing blasphemy. They were implying the same thing Christians imply today that “son of יהוה” means equal to יהוה and יהוה in the flesh. Yahushua wasn’t buying it and defended himself very well against these charges. He never defended them as Christians imply by saying he was יהוה in the flesh.

Yahushua responded to their claims of blasphemy below with my comments in red parenthesis. We see that Yahushua explained to the Yehudim who wanted to stone him for blasphemy that he used the term “son of Elohim” in the same sense that יהוה claimed all children of Yisra’el were Elohim, the son of the Most High. So, Yahushua asked them “how is it blasphemy just because I refer to myself as the son of Elohim when יהוה said we all are?”

Yochanan/John 10

29My Father, who has given them to me, is greater than all; no one can snatch them out of my Father’s hand. 30I and the Father are one in accord (in mind, will, purpose, and spirit through marriage covenant. We ALL are one with our Father in the same exact way).”

Yochanan/John 17:19-21

20“My prayer is not for them alone. I pray also for those who will believe in (enter into covenant with) me through their message, 21that all of them may be one, Father, just as You are in me and I am in You. May they also be ONE in us [one in mind, will, purpose and vision]

10:34 Yahushua answered them, “Is it not written in your Law, ‘I have said you are “elohims”’? 35 If he called them (the Yisra’elites) ‘elohim,’ to whom the word of Elohim came—and Scripture cannot be set aside— 36 what about the one whom the Father set apart as his very own and sent into the world? Why then do you accuse me of blasphemy because I said, ‘I am Elohim’s Son’? 37 Do not believe me unless I do the works of my Father. 38 But if I do them, even though you do not believe me, believe the works, that you may know and understand that the Father is in (covenant with) me, and I in (covenant with) the Father.” 39 Again they tried to seize him, but he escaped their grasp.

Yahushua’s defence was simple as he knows we are all one with יהוה and sons of יהוה not just Yahushua. He was accepting what יהוה had declared in Tehillim/Psalm 82 and quoted that Scripture back to the Yehudim. He wasn’t saying OR implying he was Elohim incarnate or יהוה in the flesh, he was simply acknowledging a fact that pertains to ALL of us…

Yahushua realized at that point that the “trap” they had laid for him was falsely accusing him of blasphemy by saying Yahushua implied he was יהוה by using the term son of Elohim. Yahushua explained that he was not saying he was יהוה. He was acknowledging the fact that, as יהוה said, he was an elohim and the son of יהוה. The same thing said of King David, Solomon, and many others in scripture. The same thing said of all those in The Covenant with Yahushua. The answer is no, Yahushua did not commit blasphemy and no Yahushua never said he was Elohim. Both the Yehudim and the Christians today are bearing false witness against the Messiah. Yahushua explained exactly what he meant.
