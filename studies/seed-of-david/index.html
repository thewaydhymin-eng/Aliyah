Key Points:

  • - יהוה "swore" this promise to David that his seed would rule the earth.
  • - The fruit of David’s body was referring to his seed, which was יהושע Messiah.
  • - David’s "branch" refers to his offspring and is the Hebrew word "tsamech".
  • - יהוה’s promise in the Tanach was always that He would one day bless all nations through one man who would be of the family of king David.
  • - Scripture reveals that this offspring of David would be a "great King", he would "live forever", and establish a kingdom "that would never end."
  • - Samu’el Bet 7 reveals that through David's family would come a line of kings, culminating in One "Eternal King" whose kingdom would never pass away.
  • -Mattitiyahu’s genealogy traces the lineage of Messiah back to King David.
  • - The King referred to in Yirmeyahu 23 is יהושע, He is to reign, and prosper, execute judgment and righteousness. Yehudah will be saved and Yisra’el will have peace.
  • - " יהוה our Righteousness" in Hebrew is יהוה Tsidkenu.
