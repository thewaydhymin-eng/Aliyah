יהוה Never Claimed That He Was the Son of Man

“Son of man” is a title for every human being. Yahushua used it of himself 87 times; יהוה never did.

1 min read2-page studyDownload PDF

Son of man is a TITLE bestowed upon all individuals of the human race.

Being the offspring of a male signifies being naturally born as a human being. The Messiah expressed his identity as a human, stating on 87 occasions that he was the son of a man.

Our Father, the divine being יהוה, never identified Himself as the Son of Man.

He consistently denied being human and expressed no intention of ever lowering himself to that level.
