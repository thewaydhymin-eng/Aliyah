Who Created the Universe?

Various Brit Chadashah (NT) verses seem to imply that The Son of יהוה, Yahushua HaMashiyach created the heavens and the earth. In these verses, we are told that THROUGH Yahushua all things were created!!!! Yet in many other passages in the Tanakh (OT) we are told that The Father יהוה ALONE created the Heavens and the Earth. Who Created The Universe, The Father, The Son or Both? Let’s study to show ourselves approved.

#G1223 is used in the Brit Chadashah verses to translate it to through. Keep in mind that our ‘Bibles’ are Hellenised. In other words, translated from Hebrew into Latin Greek, and other languages. WHICH one is it? And does this imply that THEY are ONE and the same? Let's see what the Greek word means:

G1223

διά

dia

dee-ah'

A primary preposition denoting the channel of an act; through (in very wide applications, local, causal or occasional). In composition it retains the same general import: - after, always, among, at, to avoid, BECAUSE OF (that), briefly, by, for (cause) . . . fore, from, in, by occasion of, of, BY REASON OF, FOR THE SAKE OF, that, thereby, therefore, X though, through (-out), to, wherefore, with (-in). In composition, it retains the same general import.
