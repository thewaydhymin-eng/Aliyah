Yahushua My Brother

Yahushua calls us his brothers. He is our brother, and יהוה is our Elohim and Father.

3 min read3-page studyDownload PDF

Yahushua clearly admits he is not my "Elohim" but my

Yahushua is our brother not our "Elohim". יהוה is our Elohim and Father not our brother. The simplicity and obviousness of the truth is inescapable. It was not through "Yahushua" that all things exist as the pagan "Logos" lie is taught, but through יהוה alone...

Ib’rim/Hebrews 2:10-18

10In bringing many sons and daughters to glory, it was fitting that יהוה, for whom and through whom everything exists, (יהוה) should make the pioneer of their salvation (Yahushua the forefather of eternal life) perfect through what he suffered (Yahushua was not Elohim incarnate he wasn’t even born perfect! Luqas 2:52). 11Both the one who makes people set-apart (יהוה) and those who are made set-apart (Yahushua and all his brother/sisters) are of the same family. So Yahushua is not ashamed to call them brothers and sisters. 12He says,

“I will declare your name (יהוה) to my brothers and sisters (Yahushua is not our Elohim, he is our elder brother in the family of יהוה); in the assembly I will sing your praises.” 13And again, “I (Yahushua) will put my trust in him (יהוה).” And again he says, “Here am I, and the (rest of the) children יהוה has given me (to rule over as king).”

14Since the children have flesh and blood, he too shared in their humanity (he was human too not a demi-god) so that by his death (the death of a man not Elohim) he might break the power of him who holds the power of death (defeat The Law of Sin and Death)—that is, the devil— 15and free (all who now live by The Law of the Spirit of Life) those who all their lives were held in slavery by their fear of death (we were held in slavery to the death decrees not The Law). 16For surely it is not angels Yahushua helps, but Avraham’s descendants. 17For this reason he (Yahushua) had to be made like them (he was made human just like I was), fully human in every way (doesn’t get any clearer than that), in order that he might become a merciful and faithful high priest (all High Priests are chosen from among men… Hebrews 5:1) in service to יהוה (he was not the incarnate יהוה), and that he might make atonement for the sins of the people. 18Because he himself suffered when he was tempted (proof he was not יהוה, יהוה cannot be tempted by evil), he is able to help those who are being tempted.

Ya’aqov/James 1:13

For יהוה cannot be tempted by evil, nor does he tempt anyone

That is the long and short of it. There is absolutely no difference between Yahushua pre-resurrection than there is you and I now accept Birth right, inheritance, and position in the Kingdom of יהוה. All the claims he made of being one with יהוה we too have the authority to claim. All the claims to be elohim, we too have the authority to claim. We too are the sons of יהוה. Everything that Yahushua is now post resurrection, in terms of “being”, we will be too after we are resurrected. I will be an elohim, a King, a Priest, Righteous Judge etc. Again, the main difference is that Yahushua is the first-born son, he is the King of Kings, and High Priest. WE ARE OF THE SAME EXACT FAMILY! There is no Trinity or “Godhead” there is יהוה the Father and all His chosen sons.

יהוה is the Elohim of Yahushua just like he is my Elohim!

The scripture below speaks volumes, and should be the end of this discussion:

Yochanan/John 20:17

17Yahushua said to her, "Do not cling to Me, for I have not yet ascended to My Father; but go to My brothers and say to them, 'I am ascending to My Father and your Father, and to My Elohim and your Elohim.' "

Again, it just doesn’t get any clearer than that.
